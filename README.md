# Choosing the Right Reporting Guideline for Authors and Editorial Staff

A short manual for identifying the appropriate reporting guideline for a research manuscript and using the corresponding checklist during manuscript preparation or editorial review.

## What this manual covers

The manual provides practical ways to:

* identify a reporting guideline based on study design or research type
* distinguish between commonly confused guidelines and their extensions
* compare related guidelines such as PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR, and PRISMA-DTA
* map reporting checklist items to sections of a manuscript
* record checklist items while preparing a manuscript
* perform a quick reporting-guideline check during editorial or peer review

The manual focuses on commonly used reporting guidelines and uses the EQUATOR Network as a starting point for locating guidelines and related resources.

## Scope and sources

This is a research-based technical guide derived from publicly available reporting-guideline documentation, the EQUATOR Network, and related sources. The toolkit is intended as a practical working aid; it does not replace the original reporting guideline, checklist, or journal-specific author instructions.

Reporting guidelines and their extensions can be updated, replaced, or supplemented by new guidelines. Readers should check the current version of the relevant guideline and the target journal's requirements before applying a checklist to a manuscript.
